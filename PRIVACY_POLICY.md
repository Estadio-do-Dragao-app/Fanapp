# Privacy Policy — Urban Navigation Navigator

Last Updated: May 3, 2026

Welcome to our Urban Navigation Service. We value your privacy and operate under the principle of **Privacy by Design**. This policy explains how we handle your information.

## 1. Data We Collect
*   **Approximate Location (GPS):** Collected only when the app is active and with your explicit consent.
*   **Device Identifier (UUID):** An anonymous, randomly generated ID used to distinguish your device from others without identifying you personally.
*   **Usage Metadata:** Anonymous entry/exit events at specific Points of Interest (POIs).

## 2. Purpose of Processing
We process this data to:
*   Calculate the most efficient walking routes for you.
*   Provide real-time wait time estimates for restrooms, food courts, and other services.
*   Analyze crowd density to suggest safer, less congested paths.

## 3. Legal Basis
The legal basis for processing your location data is your **Explicit Consent** (GDPR Article 6(1)(a)). You can withdraw this consent at any time through the app settings.

## 4. Data Retention (How long we keep data)
*   **Real-time GPS:** Processed in volatile memory (RAM) and **permanently deleted after 20 seconds**.
*   **Movement Events:** Stored in our database for a maximum of **24 hours** to analyze daily trends, after which they are automatically purged.
*   **Audit Logs:** Stored for accountability purposes to prove compliance with data protection laws.

## 5. Third-Party Sharing
**We do not share, sell, or rent your personal data to any third parties.** All processing is performed on our secure, private infrastructure.

## 6. Your Rights (ARCO Rights)
Under GDPR, you have the right to:
*   **Access:** Know what data we have about your device.
*   **Erasure:** Request the deletion of your data (Right to be Forgotten).
*   **Withdraw Consent:** Stop all data collection immediately by changing your app settings.

To exercise these rights, simply clear the app's cache or revoke consent within the application interface.

## 7. Security
We use industry-standard measures, including **Audit Logging** and **Service Isolation**, to ensure your data remains secure and anonymous.

---
*This service is provided as part of an academic project and follows the best practices of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).*
